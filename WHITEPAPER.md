# $WREC (WatchRec) Official Whitepaper

## 1. Executive Summary

WatchRec ($WREC) is a Web3 community-driven ecosystem powered by the BNB Smart Chain (BEP-20). Designed around interactive engagement via Telegram Mini Apps, WatchRec combines casual arcade gaming, community quests, and reward distribution mechanisms to build a scalable and sustainable decentralized ecosystem.

## 3. Ecosystem Architecture & Infrastructure

* **Telegram Mini App Engine:** Interactive gaming portal providing instant Web3 accessibility directly within Telegram.
* **Smart Contract Layer:** Audited BEP-20 token standard on BNB Smart Chain offering low gas fees and high transaction efficiency.
* **Web Portal:** Hosted on Vercel (`watchrec-airdrop.vercel.app`) integrated with Web3 provider connections.
