# WatchRec Game ($WREC) - Whitepaper

## 1. Introduction & Vision
WatchRec Game ($WREC) is an innovative Telegram Mini App gaming platform that blends retro arcade nostalgia with modern Web3 utilities. Our mission is to create an engaging, player-driven ecosystem where gaming efforts, community engagement, and ad interactions are directly rewarded through blockchain technology on the BNB Smart Chain (BSC).

## 2. Tokenomics ($WREC)
- **Token Name:** WatchRec
- **Token Symbol:** $WREC
- **Network:** BNB Smart Chain (BEP-20)
- **Contract Address:** `0x38eb8b7C8E1305fcc041E5e8E352fBB05F603Afb`
- **Utility:** 
  - In-game rewards and currency exchange (WRGP to $WREC).
  - Access to exclusive airdrops and staking features.
  - Ecosystem governance and community voting rights.

## 3. Core Gameplay Features
- **Match-3 Puzzle:** Fast-paced, engaging matching mechanics rewarding players with score multipliers and token bonuses.
- **Wolf Hit Arcade:** Classic arcade-style physics action testing reflex skills and rewarding precision hits.
- **Ad & Engagement Rewards:** Players earn continuous rewards by interacting with integrated ad networks and inviting friends through Telegram referral links.
