$WREC (WatchRec) Official Whitepaper

WatchRec ($WREC) is a community-driven Web3 ecosystem running on BNB Smart Chain (BEP-20). Built around interactive engagement through Telegram Mini Apps, it combines casual arcade gaming, community quests and reward distribution to create a scalable and sustainable decentralised ecosystem.

1. Executive Summary

WatchRec links play and earning through a real product: players open the Telegram Mini App, accumulate points (WRGP), and once the token is listed those points convert into on-chain $WREC. At the core of the ecosystem, 75% of all platform advertising and gaming revenue is distributed to players, so rewards are not drained from a fixed treasury but paid from revenue generated anew every season.

  • Smart contract layer — BEP-20 on BNB Smart Chain, low fees and fast settlement.

4. Roadmap

Phase 1 — Foundation & launch (completed): contract deployment, GitHub repository and whitepaper release, airdrop portal and Mini App integration, BscScan token info and logo verification.

The initial major airdrop is distributed to players around the token listing eve.

Phase 4 — Long-term scaling (late 2028 and beyond): extended staking and utility, treasury unlock execution (November 2028) for next-generation ecosystem development.

5. Sustainable 3-Month Airdrop & Reward Model

Once the token is listed on exchanges, the ecosystem transitions into a cyclic model. Game scores reset and players compete in 3-month competitive seasons. 75% of all advertising and gaming revenue generated by the platform is distributed as airdrops every 3 months according to leaderboard rankings.

Important: this cycle starts after listing. There is no 3-month payout calendar running today. Before listing, players accumulate points and WRGP; the first major airdrop is paid around the listing eve.

Distribution through market purchases: once all tokens are trading in the market, the rewards paid to players who play to earn airdrops are not taken from supply. At the end of every season the platform uses 75% of its ad and game revenue to buy $WREC on the open market and airdrops those tokens to players according to leaderboard ranking. The reward pool therefore does not inflate circulating supply; the model stays sustainable and regular market purchases help the token preserve its value. The purchase amount depends on season revenue and is not a fixed or guaranteed return.
